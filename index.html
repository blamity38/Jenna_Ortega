The Artistic Path

Jenna Marie Ortega, born September 27, 2002, has quickly ascended to the pinnacle of Hollywood as one of the most versatile talents of her generation. Beginning her professional career as a child actress, she displayed an innate ability to embody complex characters, eventually leading to her definitive role as the modern face of gothic drama.

With a career spanning over a decade, Jenna has seamlessly transitioned from television breakout star to a global cinematic icon. Her dedication to her craft is matched only by her commitment to activism, making her a powerful voice for her Puerto Rican and Mexican heritage and a beacon for young creators worldwide.

Wednesday

In this masterful reimagining by Tim Burton, Jenna delivers a powerhouse performance as Wednesday Addams during her formative years at Nevermore Academy. Beyond her stoic dialogue delivery, Jenna made headlines by choreographing her own viral dance sequence, turning the series into an unprecedented cultural phenomenon that dominated global charts for months.
Theatrical Masterpiece

Jenna steps into the surreal world of Tim Burton once more as Astrid Deetz, the rebellious and sharp-witted daughter of Lydia Deetz. Acting alongside industry legends, she brings a grounded, modern perspective to this gothic fantasy, cementing her reputation as the definitive muse of contemporary dark cinema.
Horror Franchise

Scream VI

Reprising her role as Tara Carpenter, Jenna takes the lead in this intense survival thriller set in the heart of New York City. Her ability to balance raw vulnerability with fierce, tactical intelligence has redefined the horror genre's "Final Girl" trope for a new era, earning her critical acclaim across the industry.
